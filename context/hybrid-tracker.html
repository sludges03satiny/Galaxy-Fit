Autoregulation Rules
🟢 GREEN
Sleep ≥7h + stress ≤5. Train as programmed. Optional top set at RPE 8–9.
🟡 YELLOW
Sleep 6–7h OR stress 6–7. Drop RPE by 1. Remove HIIT. Keep skill work.
🔴 RED
Sleep <6h OR stress ≥8. Skill + 1 main lift only. Or: 30 min Zone 1 walk + mobility.
Progression Logic
Double Progression: Each lift has a rep range (e.g. 4×5–8). When all sets hit the top (8 reps) at RPE ≤8 → increase weight next session (2.5–5kg lower, 1.25–2.5kg upper).

After 1 week off: Resume sequence. Reduce loads 10–15%. No punishment sessions.

Deload weeks: Week 5 and 10 of each block. 30–40% volume reduction. Mandatory.
